Shellui Hosting

Host your Shellui apps

shellui.app is Shellui Hosting - deploy a build and serve it on a stable subdomain. Your products run inside the shell; we handle the public URL, previews, and delivery.

Deploy

Log in once, then deploy. You get a real HTTPS origin on *.shellui.app. 

$ shellui login
Logged in as you@example.com

$ shellui deploy
Uploading build…
Deployed
q8n4m2xk7wph https://q8n4m2xk7wph.shellui.app/

How it fits

  1. 1. Build with Shellui - configure the shell, then shellui build.
  2. 2. Login and deploy - shellui login, then shellui deploy to a slug on *.shellui.app.
  3. 3. Share the URL - teammates and agents hit a real HTTPS origin, not a laptop port.

For humans and agents
